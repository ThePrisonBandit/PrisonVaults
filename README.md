🔥 Key Features
🐾 Pets Companion System (MAJOR UPDATE!)
Pets are no longer just cosmetic—they are fully functional combat companions using Minecraft 1.21's scaling technology.

Smart Combat AI: Pets protect you! They automatically attack monsters in PvE and defend you against players in PvP.

Assist Mode: If you strike an enemy, your pet attacks them.

Defense Mode: If you get hit, your pet retaliates.

Ranged & Melee Tactics:

Lil Blaze & Ghast: Shoot fireballs (Visuals only, no fire damage to blocks).

Lil Skeleton & Wither: Shoot arrows and Wither Skulls (Applies Wither effect!).

Lil Snowman: Throws snowballs to knock back enemies.

RPG Mechanics:

Health Tracking: Pets have 100 HP. Their health is displayed live in their nametag (e.g., Lil Warden 45❤).

Auto-Respawn: If your pet falls in battle, it automatically respawns near you after 10 seconds.

Creeper Scaring: Pets actively scare away Creepers to keep you safe while mining.

"Lil" Mobs: Collect tiny versions of huge monsters including the Lil Warden, Lil Iron Golem, Lil Wither, and Lil Blaze.

Domestic Pets: Collect every Cat variant, Parrots, Dogs, and Foxes (Red & Snow variants).

Safety First: Friendly Fire is disabled (you can't hurt your pet, and they can't hurt you). Pet projectiles will not break blocks or start fires.

🛡️ Staff Management & Punishments
Manage your team and keep your server safe directly from within the game.

Punishment Suite: Built-in commands for Kick, Ban, Warn, and Pardon (Unban).

Rank Safety: Owners and Co-Owners are protected—they cannot be kicked/banned by lower staff.

Staff GUI: Operators can use /staff to open a sleek GUI to manage the team.

Global Staff Mail: Use /mail staff <msg> to alert the entire team with sound notifications.

🛠️ Commands & Permissions
Player Commands
/pv <number> - Open a specific vault.

/rankup - Advance to the next prison rank.

/balance - Check your current wallet.

/pay <player> <amount> - Send money to others.

/mail <player|gang|staff> <msg> - Send mail.

/inbox - Check your personal mail.

/colorify <preset> - Change your name color/gradient style.

/pvcompass - Toggle the overhead HUD compass.

/pvinfo - View plugin version and update status.

/rob <player> - Attempt to rob a player.

Pet, Gang & Job Commands
/pets - Open your pet collection. (NEW)

/petshop - Open the pet shop to buy new companions. (NEW)

/gang create <name> <tag> - Start a new gang.

/gang invite <player> - Invite a user.

/gang manager - Open the Gang GUI.

/gangchat <msg> - Toggle or send gang chat. (NEW)

/job join <job> - Join a job.

/myprofile - View your personal stats card.

Staff & Admin Commands
/staff - (OP) Open the Staff Management GUI.

/setstaff <player> <rank> - (OP) Set a player's staff rank.

/pvperm <group|user> ... - (OP) Manage permissions.

/staffchat <msg> - (Staff) Toggle or send staff chat. (NEW)

/pvkick, /pvwarn, /pvban, /pvpardon - Punishment commands.

/staffmail read - (Staff) Read global staff announcements.

/createkit - (Admin) Create a procedural kit.
