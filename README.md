🔥 Key Features
🛡️ Staff Management & Punishments
Manage your team and keep your server safe directly from within the game.

Punishment Suite: Built-in commands for Kick, Ban, Warn, and Pardon (Unban).

Rank Safety: Owners and Co-Owners are protected—they cannot be kicked, banned, or warned by lower-ranking staff.

Zero Dependencies: You don't need LuckPerms or GroupManager to run your server.

Easy Management: Create groups, add permissions, and assign users with simple commands (/pvperm).

Inheritance: Create rank ladders easily (e.g., Admin inherits everything from Moderator).

🛠️ Commands & Permissions
Player Commands
/pv <number> - Open a specific vault.

/rankup - Advance to the next prison rank.

/balance - Check your current wallet.

/pay <player> <amount> - Send money to others.

/mail <player|gang|staff> <msg> - Send mail to a player, a gang, or the entire staff team.

/inbox - Check your personal mail.

/colorify <preset> - Change your name color/gradient style.

/pvcompass - Toggle the overhead HUD compass.

/pvinfo - View plugin version, authors, and update status. (NEW)

/rob <player> - Attempt to rob a player.

Gang & Job Commands
/gang create <name> <tag> - Start a new gang.

/gang invite <player> - Invite a user.

/gang manager - Open the GUI to manage members, colors, description, and settings.

/job join <job> - Join a job.

/myprofile - View your personal stats card.

Staff & Admin Commands
/staff - (OP) Open the Staff Management GUI.

/setstaff <player> <rank> - (OP) Directly set a player's staff rank.

/pvperm <group|user> ... - (OP) Manage permissions, groups, and inheritance.

/pvkick <player> [reason] - (Mod+) Kick a player.

/pvwarn <player> [reason] - (Mod+) Warn a player.

/pvban <player> [reason] - (Admin+) Ban a player.

/pvpardon <player> - (Admin+) Unban a player.

/staffmail read - (Staff) Read global staff announcements.

/createkit - (Admin) Create a procedural kit with abilities.

⚙️ Configuration
PrisonVaults is highly configurable!

config.yml: Configure your CurseForge API key for automatic updates.

permissions.yml: Defines groups and permissions for the built-in manager.

staff-mail.yml: Stores global staff messages safely.

kits.yml: Full control over kit contents, abilities, and cooldowns.

gangs.yml: Keeps track of all gangs, members, and descriptions.

ranks.yml: Define your rank ladder and costs.
